# Vanilla Bomj Simulator (VBS)

Student Unity project by a team of three. March 2022 – December 2023.

---

Story of a former engineer who thinks he is a genius designer, lost in conspiracy theories. Fired for alcoholism and stealing parts. Now he wants to build a flying saucer and leave people who do not understand him.

Started as a Doom-style movement experiment. Evolved into a slower, heavier game about exploring the world, not just shooting through scenery.

## Gameplay

- Combat with attack and dodge
- Slingshot for puzzles and combat
- NPCs who help set up a base
- Stats: Strength, Stamina, Speed
- Inventory: canned food and vodka for health

### Enemies

Police officer, cleaner, rat (steals resources), homeless person.

### Helpful characters

Pigeon, former boxer, engineer, thief.

## Style

Voxel world. Buildings of different heights with window details. Day/night lighting.
